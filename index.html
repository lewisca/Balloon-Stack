Balloons differ. Some cling to their neighbors, some bounce right off. Squeeze one before you place it to get a feel for it.

How to play
  • Drag a balloon off the floor and let go over the corner. It settles where you drop it.
  • If any balloon rolls out past the lip, the stack falls. Get all 11 up to win.
  • One squeeze per turn. Grippy, soft balloons stack best.
